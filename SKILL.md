# kintsugi

## Purpose
Connect distinct elements into a coherent new relationship or synthesis while preserving the identity and provenance of the inputs.

## Type
Generic cognitive-operation skill.

## Inputs
- Two or more elements, materials, observations, or states
- Optional context or constraints

## Outputs
- proposed connections
- synthesis or recombination
- preserved source relationships
- assumptions introduced by the synthesis
- alternative connections when multiple interpretations are plausible

## Procedure
1. Identify the elements and their meaningful properties.
2. Find compatible, complementary, contrasting, or otherwise useful relationships.
3. Propose one or more explicit connections.
4. Construct a synthesis without erasing the identity of the source elements.
5. Mark assumptions and newly introduced relationships.
6. Preserve provenance for all source contributions.

## Constraints
- Do not merge elements merely because they are available.
- Do not erase contradictions or differences.
- Do not present invented connections as source facts.
- Preserve provenance.
- Remain domain-agnostic.

## Domain plugin hook
A domain plugin may define acceptable synthesis patterns, vocabulary, constraints, or output schemas. It must not change the core operation: creating explicit, traceable connections between distinct elements.
